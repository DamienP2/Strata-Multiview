Strata Multiview is installed and running

The Strata Multiview icon is now in the menu bar, at the top right of the screen. It shows the server status and opens:

  • the Projects, where the multiviews are designed;
  • the Output streams;
  • the Settings: port, video format, ST 2110 and SDI outputs.

To uninstall, open Applications ▸ Strata Multiview ▸ Uninstall Strata Multiview.
Server log: /Library/Logs/Strata Multiview/strata-mv-server.log
