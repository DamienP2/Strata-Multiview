Welcome to Strata Multiview

This installs the Strata Multiview broadcast multiviewer server:

  • the server, running as a system service from startup;
  • the Strata Multiview menu bar app to control and configure it;
  • the Uninstall Strata Multiview app.

Everything goes into the Applications/Strata Multiview folder. Your data (multiviews, inputs, settings) lives in /Library/Application Support/Strata Multiview and is kept on upgrade.
